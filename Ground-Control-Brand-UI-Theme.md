# Ground Control --- Brand & UI Theme

> **Your family mission control.**

Ground Control is a family planner designed for children, teenagers and
adults. It should feel friendly, energetic and immediately
understandable without becoming childish. The visual metaphor is
**family mission control**: everyone has things happening, and Ground
Control helps the family see what is next, remember the small things and
stay coordinated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Brand Personality

Ground Control should feel:

-   **Playful, not childish**
-   **Bright, not noisy**
-   **Modern, not corporate**
-   **Warm, not sterile**
-   **Simple, not simplistic**
-   **Useful at a glance**
-   **Suitable for a child, teenager or adult**
-   **Comfortable on a phone, tablet or shared family display**

The space theme should provide personality rather than dominate the
product. Rockets, planets, stars and orbital lines are supporting brand
devices. The app is still primarily a family organiser.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Core Brand Idea

### Ground Control

The family is the mission.

Events, school, sport, reminders, money, medication, birthdays,
holidays, chores and small everyday notes all orbit around the family.

Ground Control brings them together in one place.

### Suggested straplines

**Primary:**\
\> Your family mission control.

**Alternative:**\
\> Plan together. Stay on track. Launch every day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Logo System

The primary identity combines:

1.  A rounded-square app icon
2.  A small rocket launching from a moon/planet
3.  A deep space-purple background
4.  The **Ground Control** wordmark

### Logo variants

Create and maintain:

-   Primary horizontal logo
-   Stacked logo
-   Icon-only mark
-   Light-background version
-   Dark-background version
-   Monochrome version
-   Small favicon/PWA version

The rocket mark should remain recognisable at small sizes. Avoid
excessive detail in production versions.

### Colour Philosophy

Purple and deep navy form the core Ground Control identity.

Pink, amber, mint and blue are supporting colours used for:

-   Family members
-   Event categories
-   Status
-   Notes
-   Visual differentiation

Do not give every component a different colour. Most screens should
remain predominantly white/light with controlled colour accents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Typography

### Recommended Typeface

**Poppins**

Weights:

-   Bold --- hero headings
-   SemiBold --- titles and important labels
-   Medium --- buttons and navigation
-   Regular --- body text

### Hierarchy

-   Hero greeting: large, bold and friendly
-   Page title: bold
-   Section heading: uppercase or semi-bold with generous tracking
-   Card title: semi-bold
-   Body: regular
-   Metadata: medium/smaller
-   Navigation: medium

Avoid tiny text. This is a family utility and should be readable quickly
from a kitchen counter or shared tablet.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Graphic Language

### Orbit

The orbit is a recurring Ground Control graphic device.

Use:

-   Small planets
-   Orbiting dots
-   Subtle orbital lines
-   Stars and sparkles
-   Launch trails
-   Soft space gradients

Family members can conceptually appear as objects orbiting the shared
family space.

Keep decorative elements subtle within functional screens.

### Illustration Style

Illustrations should be:

-   Rounded
-   Friendly
-   Colourful
-   Modern
-   Softly dimensional
-   Consistent with the app icon

Avoid highly realistic imagery or overly childish cartoon styling.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Family Identity

Each family member should have:

-   Initial or avatar
-   Name
-   Assigned accent colour
-   Optional profile image
-   Consistent visual identity throughout the app

Example:

  Person              Example Colour
  ------------------- ---------------------
  Finn                Sky Blue
  Nicole              Orbit Pink
  Lauren              Mission Purple
  Parent 1            Family Mint
  Parent 2            Launch Amber
  Everyone / Family   Ground Control Navy

Users should be able to customise these assignments.

## 9. Core UI Components

### Cards

Cards should use:

-   Large corner radius
-   White or softly tinted surfaces
-   Very light shadows
-   Strong title hierarchy
-   Clear metadata
-   Optional colour/category stripe

Avoid excessive glassmorphism.

### Sticky Notes

Sticky notes represent informal family information such as:

-   "Finn needs €5 Friday"
-   "Bring school form"
-   "3 weeks of tablets"
-   "6 sleeps until holidays"

They should feel more informal than calendar events.

Recommended styling:

-   Warm paper background
-   Slight rotation may be used sparingly
-   Pin/orbit-dot visual
-   Large readable text
-   Family identifier
-   Optional expiry/completion action

### Buttons

Primary: - Mission Purple - White text - Rounded - Strong but not
oversized

Secondary: - Light tinted surface - Brand-coloured text/icon

### Navigation

Primary mobile navigation:

-   Today
-   My Week
-   Add
-   Profile

The current location should be obvious through colour and shape, not
just text.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Today Screen

The Today screen should answer three questions immediately:

1.  **What is happening next?**
2.  **What do I need to remember?**
3.  **What does the rest of my day/week look like?**

Recommended order:

-   Greeting
-   Next Up
-   See My Week
-   Remember This
-   Tasks / reminders
-   Optional family activity

Personalisation should make the screen feel like **my view of the
family**, not a generic household calendar.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11. My Week

The weekly view should avoid becoming a dense traditional calendar.

Prefer:

-   Day strip across the top
-   Selected day highlighted
-   Chronological event list
-   Family colour indicators
-   Clear all-day events
-   Fast filtering by person

A shared-family mode should allow all family members to be viewed
together.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 12. Add Flow

Adding something should be extremely fast.

Primary choices:

-   Event
-   Task
-   Note
-   Reminder

Then progressively reveal only the information required.

A user should be able to create a basic note or reminder in seconds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 13. Shared Display / Kitchen Mode

Ground Control should eventually support a tablet or wall-display mode.

This view should prioritise:

-   Today
-   Who is where
-   Next events
-   Important reminders
-   Countdown items
-   Family notices

Text and touch targets should be larger than the mobile interface.

This mode is the closest digital equivalent to the family
whiteboard/fridge planner.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 14. Motion

Motion should reinforce the mission-control theme without slowing the
UI.

Examples:

-   Very small launch animation after adding an event
-   Orbit movement during loading
-   Soft bounce/check animation on task completion
-   Tiny sparkle after completing a family goal

Animations should generally complete in under one second.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15. Accessibility

Ground Control must not rely on colour alone.

Use:

-   Icons
-   Labels
-   Initials
-   Patterns/status indicators where appropriate
-   High text contrast
-   Large touch targets
-   Scalable text

Family colours should remain distinguishable but are secondary to
labels.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 16. Tone of Voice

Copy should be concise, human and positive.

Prefer:

-   **Next up**
-   **Remember this**
-   **For you**
-   **See my week**
-   **What's happening?**
-   **All sorted**
-   **Nothing else today**

Avoid corporate phrases such as:

-   Dashboard
-   Resource
-   Workflow
-   Item management
-   Productivity
-   User allocation

Ground Control should sound like something used at home.

## 18. Design Principle

> **Ground Control is not a calendar with a space theme. It is a shared
> family space that happens to organise the calendar too.**

The product should preserve the speed and informality of writing
something on the fridge while adding the advantages of a digital family
planner.

The interface should always favour **glanceability, personality and low
effort** over feature density.
